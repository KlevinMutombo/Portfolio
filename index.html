About Me

I'm Klevin Mutombo, a bilingual (English/French) Software Engineering student at the University of Ottawa (expected May 2028). I like building things end to end, from the database and auth layer up to a clean, responsive interface, and I care about code that is tested, readable, and easy for a team to maintain.

I'm currently a Software Developer (Co-op) at Statistics Canada, where I migrate legacy SAS pipelines to Python and R with test-driven development, built a 5-stage LLM-agent pipeline that drafts and validates SAS-to-Python conversions, and sped up a core workflow about 13x (134 s → 10 s). Before that, I spent two co-op terms there as a Geomatics Analyst automating spatial workflows with ArcPy, and a year as the full-stack web developer for the Telfer Business Technology Association.

Outside of work, I launched Momenta (momentafocus.com), a gamified focus app built with Next.js, React Native and Supabase, and built Recipe Hero with a team of four in 24 hours at a hackathon.
